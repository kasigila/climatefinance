Climate Finance Service

Gender Assessment

We assess how climate projects affect women, men, youth, and vulnerable groups so finance reaches communities equitably and meets funder safeguards.

What We Deliver

Gender baseline and inclusion analysis

Women economic empowerment opportunities

Risk mitigation for unequal access or harm

Gender action plans for climate funders

Why It Matters

Climate shocks and financing barriers are not gender neutral. Gender-smart design improves outcomes, repayment, resilience, and accountability.

Where acronyms are used for funder documentation, we define them clearly: Concept Notes (CNs) are early project summaries, Project Preparation Facilities (PPFs) support detailed preparation, and Funding Proposals (FPs) are full investment submissions.
