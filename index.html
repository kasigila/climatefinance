Climate Change Challenge

Climate change is already happening: temperatures are rising, drought and wildfires occur more frequently, and sea levels are rising.

Africa contributes less than 4% of global emissions—yet faces disproportionate climate impacts. Climate finance is essential to build resilience.

The IPCC report shows emissions continue to rise and current plans fall short of limiting warming to 1.5°C.

Our climate-smart microfinance approach

Changing the Finance, Financing the Change

We aim to provide innovative financial mitigation and adaptation solutions to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and help address local sustainable development goals.

Climate finance—from public, private, and alternative sources—is critical for mitigation and adaptation. Large-scale investments are required to significantly reduce emissions and help societies adapt.

  • Innovative financial solutions for climate action
  • De-risking and scaling climate investment
  • Partnerships for sustainable development
