Climate Finance Service

Microfinance for Climate

We design and support climate-smart microfinance products that help vulnerable communities access affordable capital for adaptation, resilience, and green livelihoods.

What We Deliver

Community lending product design

Borrower readiness and financial literacy support

Climate-smart agriculture and enterprise use cases

Portfolio monitoring for adaptation impact

Why It Matters

Microfinance connects global climate ambition with household-level decisions: seeds, livestock, irrigation, processing, and resilient income.

Where acronyms are used for funder documentation, we define them clearly: Concept Notes (CNs) are early project summaries, Project Preparation Facilities (PPFs) support detailed preparation, and Funding Proposals (FPs) are full investment submissions.
