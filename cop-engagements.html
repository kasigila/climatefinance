International Engagement

COP30 Brazil — Belém 2025

A Financial Mandate for the Green Decade

This global summit confirmed a profound shift, establishing explicit, massive financial targets that define the next decade of green investment. CRDB Bank Plc was well represented, showcasing the commitment of leading African financial institutions to the global climate agenda.

  • Baku-to-Belém Roadmap: Unlock at least $1.3 Trillion annually in climate finance for developing nations by 2035.
  • Tripling adaptation finance — estimated scale nearing $120 billion annually for resilience measures.

Climate action has transitioned from a passive risk concern to a primary investment strategy for the financial sector, offering both systematic risk mitigation and major, high-growth opportunities.

International Engagement

The Dialogue on Climate Adaptation in Africa at COP30

Transformative approaches for adaptation planning in Africa

Kenneth Kasigila represented CRDB Bank Plc at COP30 in Belém, Brazil, in the high-level panel "Transformative approaches for adaptation planning in Africa: Experiences and Lessons." Hosted by GCA and CGIAR at the Food & Agriculture Pavilion, the session demonstrated how the African private sector is embedding climate resilience into its core strategy—moving beyond planning to accelerate tangible investments for food security and livelihoods.

  • Session: Transformative approaches for adaptation planning in Africa: Experiences and Lessons
  • Date: 11 November 2025 · 14:00–15:00 -03
  • Location: Pavilion PV-E170, Blue Zone, Belém, Brazil
  • Speakers: Kenneth Kasigila (CRDB Bank), Anju Sharma, Kelvin Shikuku, Bernard Kimoro, Oluyede Ajayi

LinkedIn — Session details · Additional context

On Green Taxonomy and Tanzania

The discussion highlighted the operational difficulties of creating green products without a national taxonomy. Tanzania is on track to develop its own—a vital step toward unlocking green capital efficiently.

International Engagement

COP29 Azerbaijan — Baku 2024

At COP29, our team participated in side events at the Tanzania Pavilion in Baku on Finance Day, as part of the Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action (November 14, 2024). The focus: best practices and challenges for allocating and mobilizing financing for Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs).

Kenneth Kasigila shared CRDB Bank's achievements, including collaboration with MUFG Bank via Green Climate Fund projects FP223 (US$1.5b) and FP179 (US$200m), plus CRDB Bank's broader ESG journey. The insights gained inform the COP29 Action Agenda and ongoing discussions toward COP30.
