Green Climate Fund Readiness Programme Workshop

Date to be announced • Dar es Salaam, Tanzania

This capacity-building workshop supports project proponents, government agencies, and financial institutions in navigating the Green Climate Fund (GCF) accreditation and project development process.

Topics Covered

  • GCF concept note and funding proposal requirements
  • Accreditation pathways for Direct Access Entities
  • Climate rationale and logical framework development
  • Stakeholder engagement and gender assessment
  • Financial modelling and risk assessment
