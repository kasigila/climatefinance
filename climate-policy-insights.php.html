2025UNDP · Tanzania National BIOFIN Technical Committee

Member, Tanzania National BIOFIN Technical Committee

Kenneth Davis Kasigila serves on Tanzania's National Biodiversity Finance Initiative (BIOFIN) Technical Committee under UNDP — connecting biodiversity finance policy with climate finance implementation on the ground.

This role bridges national biodiversity finance planning with the microfinance and GCF project work Africa Climate Finance delivers across Tanzania's Southern Highlands.

Why this matters

  • BIOFIN aligns biodiversity and climate finance at the national policy level
  • Connects institutional frameworks to grassroots microfinance delivery
  • Strengthens Tanzania's capacity to mobilize finance for nature and climate
